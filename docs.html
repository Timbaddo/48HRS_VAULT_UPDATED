How 48HRS VAULT works

1. Create your account

Sign up with Firebase authentication and sync your account with the platform.

2. Connect WhatsApp

Use the bot pairing flow to connect your WhatsApp number. Your trial is tracked by the backend.

3. Use your trial

Your free trial lasts 24 hours. Actual bot usage is recorded separately from trial expiration.

4. Upgrade

When your trial ends, activate VIP from the dashboard through the available payment flow.

5. Refer friends

Qualified referrals can unlock 1-month VIP at 3 referrals and lifetime VIP at 10 referrals.
